# Code of conduct

This is a small project and the rules are short.

Be straightforward and be kind. Disagree with the work, not the person.
Assume the other party is acting in good faith until shown otherwise, and
when you are shown otherwise, say so plainly and without contempt.

Specifically, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else this
project is represented:

- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- No publishing of others' private information.
- No sustained disruption of a conversation after being asked to stop.
- Reviews criticize code and claims. They do not speculate about the
  author's competence or motives.
- A false-positive report is the most valuable thing a stranger can give
  this project. Treat one as a gift, even when it turns out to be a
  misreading.

If you see behavior that breaks these rules, email
**keith.adler@icloud.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept
confidential, and acted on: a warning, a temporary block, or a permanent
one, depending on what happened. Maintainers are held to the same
standard as everyone else.
